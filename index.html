About

Background

I'm a Computer Science student at Clemson University with a background that started outside of software, in the trades rather than the classroom. That path taught me precision, discipline, and a habit of thinking in systems, qualities I now bring directly to my work in robotics and software engineering.

I'm currently a research assistant in Clemson's AI SENDS Lab (AI based Sensing, Networking, and Data Services), led by Dr. Abolfazl Razi, where I work on interfacing vision language models and vision transformers into robotics applications. I'm especially drawn to system level programming and building software that bridges perception and control in real world environments.

Outside the lab, I chase the same curiosity through audio engineering, and enjoy understanding how systems actually work well enough to make them work better.

Lab: AI SENDS Lab, Clemson University — currently reading into tactile feedback methods for vision language action models, including work like VLA Touch.
