About

I'm a Computer Science student at Clemson University and a research assistant in Clemson's AI SENDS Lab (AI based Sensing, Networking, and Data Services), led by Dr. Abolfazl Razi. My work there focuses on getting vision language models and vision transformers to work inside robotics applications.

I'm especially interested in system level programming and in building software that connects perception to control in real world environments. My projects include reinforcement learning for autonomous drone navigation and a simulation environment for tactile, contact rich robotic manipulation.

Outside of research, I work in audio engineering, producing and mixing music and building tools like TrackTweak, a real time plugin that flags mix issues.

The AI SENDS Lab builds intelligent systems that can sense, communicate, reason, and act together in uncertain, resource-limited environments, from autonomous vehicles and drones to edge devices and assistive tools. Its research ties together information theory, machine learning, and large and vision language models, and it is supported by NSF, the US Air Force Research Laboratory, USDA, BMW Research Group, MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Clemson VIPR (DoD), SC EPSCoR, and the Arizona Commerce Authority.

Robotic manipulation simulation

Ongoing

Built a simulation environment for contact rich robotic manipulation, modeling tactile and force/torque sensor feedback across configurable object materials to test a grip adjustment algorithm in a closed loop, repeatable testbed.

Robotics Simulation, Tactile Sensing

Part of the Anomaly Detection for Resilient Autonomy-Enabled Vehicle Systems research project at the AI SENDS Lab, led by Principal Investigator Dr. Abolfazl Razi and funded by US Army / VIPR-GS. The broader project processes driving trajectories, from online sources or dashboard cameras, to model and identify anomalous actions, using sequential learning (e.g., ViT, LSTM), trajectory extraction by GIS mapping, inverse learning to reconstruct reward maps, and a supervised framework for deviation analysis and anomaly detection.
